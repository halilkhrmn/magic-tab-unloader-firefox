# Privacy Policy

Magic Tab Unloader does not collect, transmit or share any data.

- All settings and unload statistics are stored locally in your browser (`storage.local`).
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or remote code.
- Tab URLs and titles are read only inside your browser to decide when to unload a tab. They are never sent anywhere.
- To unload tabs again after a browser restart, the extension keeps a list of the URLs of the tabs that are currently unloaded in `storage.local`. It stays on your computer, and the option can be switched off in the settings.
- If you enable "Grey out the icons of tabs this extension unloads", the extension asks for the optional "access all sites" permission. It is used only to fetch a tab's own favicon, redraw it in grey in the browser and set it on that tab. Nothing is sent to any server, and unticking the option removes the permission.

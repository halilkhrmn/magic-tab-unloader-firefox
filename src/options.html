Tab appearance

Firefox greys out tabs unloaded from its own right-click menu, but not tabs unloaded by an extension. This option swaps the page icon for a grey, half-transparent copy just before the tab is unloaded. It needs permission to access all sites (Firefox asks when you tick the box); the icon is only read and redrawn on your computer.

Alternatively, open about:config and set browser.tabs.fadeOutUnloadedTabs to true to let Firefox grey out every unloaded tab. Extensions cannot change that setting.
